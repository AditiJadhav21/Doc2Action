ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Purpose:
Identify who is eligible or not eligible according to the document.

What it should contain:
- Age requirement
- Education/qualification
- Minimum marks/percentage
- Year/semester requirement
- Category requirement
- Income requirement
- Location/residency requirement
- Work/experience requirement
- Gender or other stated criteria
- Any additional eligibility conditions

If information is not available:
No specific information found in the document.
