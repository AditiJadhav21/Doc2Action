DEADLINES & KEY DATES

Purpose:
Extract every important date or time-related deadline from the document.

What it should contain:
- Application deadline
- Submission deadline
- Registration date
- Exam/test date
- Event date
- Interview date
- Verification date
- Renewal date
- Any other important date
- Date + what the date is for
- Time, if mentioned

If information is not available:
No specific information found in the document.
