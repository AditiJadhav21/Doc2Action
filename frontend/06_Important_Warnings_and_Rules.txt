IMPORTANT WARNINGS & RULES

Purpose:
Highlight instructions that the user must not miss.

What it should contain:
- Important rules
- Restrictions
- Mandatory conditions
- Penalties
- Rejection conditions
- Incorrect-information warnings
- Submission restrictions
- Document-format restrictions
- Important terms and conditions
- Any instruction marked as important, mandatory, prohibited, or required

If information is not available:
No specific information found in the document.
