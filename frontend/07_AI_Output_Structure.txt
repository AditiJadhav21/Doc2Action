DOC2ACTION AI OUTPUT STRUCTURE

The AI should convert every uploaded document into these six sections:

1. Executive Summary
   - Short overall summary

2. Deadlines & Key Dates
   - Important dates and what each date is for

3. Eligibility Criteria
   - Requirements for eligibility

4. Required Documents
   - Documents/certificates/proofs needed

5. Recommended Next Steps
   - Practical actions the user should take, in order

6. Important Warnings & Rules
   - Rules, restrictions, warnings, and mandatory instructions

GENERAL RULES

- Extract information only from the uploaded document.
- Do not invent missing information.
- Keep the output concise and easy to understand.
- Preserve important dates, numbers, names, and requirements accurately.
- If a section has no relevant information, return:
  "No specific information found in the document."
